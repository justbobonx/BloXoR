
Hello BloXoR	To complete a level, you must line up the <img src="blx-O.png" width=18 height=18>, <img src="blx-X.png" width=18 height=18>, and <img src="blx-O.png" width=18 height=18> Blox by titling the device and sliding the Blox around.
Step Two	With a little effort, you can rearrange Blox.  Remember, you have to line up the <img src="blx-O.png" width=18 height=18><img src="blx-X.png" width=18 height=18><img src="blx-O.png" width=18 height=18> Blox in that order.  (They can be lined up horizontally or vertically.)
A Twist	Bring up the <b>Pause Menu</b> at any time by tapping the Back or Menu buttons.  From the pause menu, you can Resume or Restart the current level, or Exit back to the Main Menu.
Obstacles	Too easy? We'll throw in some <img src="blx-generic-movable.png" width=18 height=18> Blox. They don't do anything.  They just slide around and generally get in your way.
Curves	Now you're getting the hang of it.  Keep an eye on what all the Blox are doing.
A Drop in the Bucket	The score is based on how much time you take as well as how many Tilts it takes you to complete a level.
Follow the Leader	Blox not quite in line?  Try tapping the edge of the device.
Break Stuff	Stuck? Look for a cracked Wall <img src="blx-breakable.png" width=18 height=18> Blox.  It can be blown up by a Bomb <img src="blx-bomb.png" width=18 height=18> Blox.  Maneuver the Bomb Blox into position and then <b>tap</b> it to detonate.  Bomb Blox can also blow up other Bomb Blox, creating chain reactions.
One Way Streets	One-Way <img src="blx-oneway-right.png" width=18 height=18> Blox let Blox slide through them in the direction the arrow indicates.  Be careful, sometimes once you go in, you can't get back out.
Dominoes	Sometimes you'll find Inactive Bomb <img src="blx-bomb-inactive.png" width=18 height=18> Blox.  They can not be detonated by themselves, but can be blown up by adjacent Bomb <img src="blx-bomb.png" width=18 height=18> Blox, and work great in chain reactions.
A Hole New Ballgame	Watch out for <b>Holes</b>!  They looke like this. <img src="blx-hole.png" width=18 height=18> Blox can fall in and they don't come out.  If you lose an essential Blox, you'll have to start the level over. (Use the Pause Menu.)
Catwalk	Blox can hang partially over a hole without falling in.  Use that to your advantage.
Bridging the Gaps	Too many holes? Use a Bridge <img src="blx-bridge-pot.png" width=18 height=18> Blox to create a  bridge over a hole, like this <img src="blx-bridge-on-a-hole.png" width=18 height=18> 
Flotsam	Sometimes holes can be used to get rid of unwanted Blox.
Bullseye	Having a Blox stuck in the wrong position is as bad as losing it down a Hole.  Remember, you can always restart from the pause menu.
All for One	Let's put it all together.  Take things one step at a time.
