What to do:
 Pick an open level from the list, and start it up. Tilt your device in the direction you want the Blox to slide. Keep sliding them around until you can bring the together. That's how you complete a level.

You can pause a level at any time by tapping the Back or Menu buttons. From the pause menu, you can Resume, Restart the level, or give up and return to the Main Menu.

Click to toggle the sound on and off.


Blox Types:
- These are the BloXoR BloXs. Line them up to complete the level.

- These are Wall Blox, they don't slide. They get in your way, but can also be useful for manipulting the position of Blox around them.

- These Blox do nothing. They are there to slide around and get in your way.

- These are Holes. Blox can and will fall through these holes, gone for good. If a needed Blox falls down a hole, you'll have to start the level over.

- These are Bridge Blox. If you can maneuver them into a hole, they will form a bridge there: , allowing Blox to slide over the hole.

- These are Wall Blox, but only one-way. Blox can slide through these, but not against the arrow.

- These are Wall Blox, but they're cracked, unstable. A Bomb Blox can be used to blow them up and get them out of the way.

- These are Bomb Blox. Tap them to detonate. They will blow up themselves as well as any breakable Wall Blox or other Bomb Blox that are adjacent to them.

- These Bomb Blox are inactive. They cannot be detonated by themselves, but will blow up when detonated by another Bomb.

- Sometimes a normally movable Blox will be bolted down. It cannot slide like that, but all of its other behaviors remain active.
